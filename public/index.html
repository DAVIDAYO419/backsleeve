Backsleeve

Paste a Spotify album link and get a print-ready poster.

Works with open.spotify.com links, spotify:album: URIs, short spotify.link share links and the bare 22 character ID.

Template

A code holds your text and style choices, so you can come back to a poster later. Cover art is fetched again from the Spotify link. Covers you uploaded are not stored.
